# Backlink and citation plan

Backlinks are links to completedeckandfence.com from other websites. They come from real listings, real partners, and content worth linking to. Nothing in the code can create them, so this is a checklist of actions, in the order that pays off fastest for a local deck and fence company.

Use the exact same business name, phone, and address (or service area) everywhere. Consistency matters as much as the links themselves.

## Week one: the listings Google trusts most

1. **Google Business Profile.** The single biggest local ranking factor. Primary category "Deck builder", secondary "Fence contractor". Add every city from `src/data/cities.json` as a service area. Link the website field to the home page. Post real project photos as soon as there are any.
2. **Google Search Console.** Verify the domain, submit `https://completedeckandfence.com/sitemap-index.xml`, and request indexing for the home page, `/decks/`, `/fences/`, and the Murfreesboro pages first.
3. **Bing Places for Business** and **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Import straight from the Google profile. Submit the same sitemap.
4. **Apple Business Connect.** Puts the business in Apple Maps and Siri results.

## Week two: the sister company link

Parker Construction Co. already ranks for deck and fence searches across the same 21 cities. That makes a link from parkerconstructioncompany.com the most relevant backlink this site can get. Decide how the two sites split the work before adding it:

* **If Complete Deck & Fence takes over decks and fences:** on Parker's `/decks/` and `/fencing/` pages and its deck and fence guides, add a short line that deck and fence projects are handled by its sister company, Complete Deck & Fence, with a link. Over time, point Parker's fencing city pages to the matching `/fence-company-{city}-tn/` pages here.
* **If both keep offering them:** add one mention on Parker's About page, "Our sister company, Complete Deck & Fence, builds decks and fences across Middle Tennessee", linked to the home page.

Either way, use the brand name as the link text, not "fence company Murfreesboro". Sitewide footer links with keyword anchors between sites with one owner look manipulative to Google. Never copy text between the two sites.

## Weeks two to four: citations

Claim each free listing, fill in every field, and link to the home page:

* Yelp
* Your regional Better Business Bureau
* Angi and HomeAdvisor
* Houzz (a strong fit for decks and outdoor living)
* Thumbtack
* Nextdoor business page
* Facebook business page and Instagram
* Porch
* Yellow Pages (yp.com) and Manta

## Ongoing: local links that are earned

* **Chambers of commerce.** Join the chamber in your home county. Member directories link to your site, and chamber links carry local weight.
* **Home Builders Association of Middle Tennessee.** Member directory listing.
* **Manufacturer contractor locators.** Decking and fencing brands list certified installers on their websites. Only list a brand after you are actually certified with it.
* **Suppliers.** Ask the lumber yards and fence supply houses you buy from whether they list contractors.
* **HOA vendor lists.** Many HOAs keep a list of contractors who know their review process. The HOA approval guide on this site is a good thing to share with them.
* **Sponsorships.** Youth sports teams and community events usually link to their sponsors.
* **Reviews.** Ask every customer for a Google review after the job. Reviews drive map rankings more than any single link.

## What never to do

* Buy links, join link networks, or pay for "SEO directory" packages.
* Create a lot of low quality directory listings at once.
* Use the same keyword anchor text over and over.
* Copy pages from parkerconstructioncompany.com or any other site.

## How to know it is working

Check Search Console monthly: pages indexed, impressions, and the queries each city page shows up for. Links from the steps above will show up under Links. Expect city pages to take a few weeks to a few months to settle into rankings. That timing is normal for a new domain.
